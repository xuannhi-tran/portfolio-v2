About

I'm Nhi, a third-year Software Engineering student at the University of Sydney. I like building small, practical web apps, and I'm looking for a software internship where I can keep learning from people who build things for real users.

Most of what I've built started with a small annoyance. The free Discord music bots all started charging, so I made one for my friends' server. I kept running out of money before the end of the month, so I built an expense tracker. As an international student, I got tired of reading a whole job ad only to find out they wanted PR or citizens, which became JobCompass, a hackathon project where I built the full app and a teammate prepared the dataset we checked it against.

I'm still early in my career, so I'd rather be straight about it: I can take an idea to a working, deployed app, and I pick up whatever a project needs. That's why my projects use a mix of React, FastAPI, Django, Next.js and Node.

Outside of code, I read a lot, mostly philosophy and psychology, and there's almost always music playing while I work. I like trying to understand why people think and act the way they do. The vinyl theme comes from the music side of that: every record here is a project.
