Hello

I'm Nhi, a third-year Software Engineering student at the University of Sydney. I like building small, practical web apps, and I'm looking for a software internship where I can keep learning from people who build things for real users.

What I build

Most of my projects started with a small annoyance: free Discord music bots that started charging, budgets that never lasted the month, and job ads that only mention "PR or citizens" at the very end. So I built a music bot, an expense tracker and JobCompass.

Outside of code

I read a lot, mostly philosophy and psychology, and there's almost always music on while I work. That's where the vinyl theme comes from.

How I work

I'm still early, so I'd rather be straight about it. JobCompass was a hackathon project where I built the full app and a teammate prepared the dataset we checked it against. I can take an idea to a working, deployed app and pick up what a project needs, which is why I've used React, FastAPI, Django, Next.js and Node.
